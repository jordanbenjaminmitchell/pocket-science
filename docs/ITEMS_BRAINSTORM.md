# Items: brainstorm for the next pass

Nothing here is built yet. It's a proposal to react to.

## What's wrong now

Most of the 13 items are a number with a label: +1 Chalk, +2 Heat, ×2 next shot, every pocket hot, pockets 20% wider. They work, but you never *look at the table* differently because of them. The good ones (Portal Pair, Pop-up Wall, Blink, Second Strike) all share one thing: **they change the geometry of the table, and you can see the result on the prediction line.** That's the thread to pull.

## The frame: the Hustler's Kit

Items become **real pool-hall props that each bend one physical rule**: chalk, a bridge, the triangle, a coin for the table, a felt brush, a scuffed old ball. They're grounded and readable, not mad science, and each one has one clear verb.

Two other frames I weighed:

- **Everything is time** (rewind, freeze, slow-mo, echo). Very cohesive with pausing, but too narrow for 20+ items and samey after a while.
- **Marks on the felt** (lines and rings you draw that change balls crossing them). Gorgeous with the prediction line, but abstract on its own.

The Kit absorbs the best of both. A few props *are* time tricks or felt marks, but they're objects you'd find in a pool hall.

## The rules every item follows

1. **One physical verb.** An item moves, adds, removes or changes something on the table. It never just adds a number. Score comes from what you then do with the table.
2. **It previews on the prediction line.** Hover or hold an item and the line redraws as if it were already used. You see a Trapdoor swallow the 5 *before* you spend it. This is the single biggest cohesion win, and it leans on the game's best feature.
3. **One of three moments**, each with a colour on the item:
   - **Set-up** (blue): while aiming.
   - **Mid-shot** (green): while a shot is paused.
   - **Aftermath** (amber): after the balls stop, before your next shot.
4. **One of two lifespans:**
   - **Tools** (2 slots, pricier, rarer): keep them all run. One charge per table, shown as a pip.
   - **Consumables** (3 slots, cheap): one use.

   Tools give your run an identity; consumables are your tricks.
5. **Items never name each other.** No "if you also have X". Perks may talk about *kinds* of items ("Tools get two charges per table"), never a specific one.
6. **They touch the core systems through the table.** The 8-ball, solids and stripes, Chain, Heat and pockets matter because of where balls end up, not because the item text mentions them.

## The proposed set (21)

### Set-up (while aiming)

| Item | Kind | What it does | Why it's interesting |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Called Shot** | Consumable | Tap a ball and a pocket. If that ball drops there this shot, it pays ×3. If not, nothing. | Pool's oldest rule. Pure skill, and the line shows whether your call is on. |
| **Trapdoor** | Consumable | Open a seventh pocket anywhere along a cushion for one shot. | Turns hopeless layouts into pots and reshapes how you see the rails. |
| **Ghost Chalk** | Consumable | Mark one ball. This shot, every other ball passes straight through it. | Removes a blocker for one shot, or lets you aim through the 8 safely. |
| **Swap** | Tool | Swap two balls' positions (not the cue ball). | A puzzle every table: which swap opens the most? |
| **Polish** | Consumable | One ball glides with no cloth friction until it first hits something. | Long banks and lazy rolls become reachable, and you can see it run on the line. |
| **Lead Weight** | Tool | One ball is four times heavier for this shot. | Becomes a backstop the cue ball can't move, or a wrecking ball if struck. |
| **Spotter** | Consumable | Return a ball you sank this table to the foot spot. It can pay again. Never the 8. | Your best ball comes back for another trip. |

### Mid-shot (while paused)

| Item | Kind | What it does | Why it's interesting |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Second Strike** | Tool | Hit the cue ball again, mid-roll. | Kept. Already the signature move. |
| **Pop-up Wall** | Consumable | A short wall for the rest of the shot. | Kept. |
| **Blink** | Consumable | Move the cue ball anywhere, keeping its speed and direction. | Kept. |
| **Freeze Frame** | Consumable | Stop one moving ball dead. It becomes a post for the rest of the shot. | Save a ball heading for the wrong pocket, or plant a backboard. |
| **Nudge** | Tool | Push any one ball a short distance in a chosen direction. | Small and precise: finishing a ball that stopped on the jaw. |
| **English Dial** | Consumable | Add side or draw to the cue ball mid-roll, so it curves from here. | Make the cue ball bend around a blocker you only noticed while paused. |
| **Gravity Pebble** | Consumable | Drop a small well that bends rolling balls for the rest of the shot. | A soft, curving version of a wall; the line shows the bend. |

### Aftermath (balls stopped)

| Item | Kind | What it does | Why it's interesting |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rewind** | Tool | Undo your last shot entirely: balls, score and Chalk. | The ultimate safety net. One charge per table keeps it honest. |
| **Insurance Slip** | Consumable | If your last shot missed, take the Chalk back. | A salvage you choose after seeing the result, not a blind +1. |
| **Triangle** | Consumable | Re-rack every ball still on the table and break again. | Fresh Rack, renamed and moved to the right moment. |
| **Ball in Hand** | Consumable | Pick up the cue ball and place it anywhere. | Kept: classic pool. |

### Any time, lasting all table

| Item | Kind | What it does | Why it's interesting |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Post** | Consumable | Place a bumper anywhere. | Bumper Kit, renamed. |
| **Portal Pair** | Consumable | Two linked portals. | Kept. |
| **Pocket Cover** | Tool | Cover one pocket for this table; the pocket opposite opens 30% wider. | You reshape the table to suit your set, instead of "pockets +20%". |

## What happens to today's 13

- **Kept:** Second Strike (becomes a Tool), Pop-up Wall, Blink, Portal Pair, Ball in Hand.
- **Renamed or moved:** Bumper Kit → Post. Fresh Rack → Triangle, now an Aftermath item.
- **Replaced:**
  - Hand Chalk → Insurance Slip (a salvage you choose, not a blind +1).
  - Double Down → Called Shot (×2 for nothing → ×3 for a call you make).
  - Spotlight → Trapdoor (a hot pocket → a new pocket).
  - Magnet Charm → Gravity Pebble (you choose where the pull is).
  - Wide Open → Pocket Cover (bigger pockets → reshape the table).
  - Heat Pack → cut. Heat should come from shots, not a pack. Freeze Frame takes its slot.

## Systems around the items

- **Ghost preview everywhere.** In the shop, hovering an item card shows a tiny animated diagram of its verb. That also guides the card art later.
- **A visible mark for every item in play.** A chalk-white ring around a frozen ball, a dashed outline on a Trapdoor, the felt darkened under a Gravity Pebble. One visual language, so a glance tells you what's active.
- **Generic item Perks only:**
  - Tools get 2 charges per table.
  - Consumables have a 1 in 4 chance not to be used up.
  - Using any item while paused adds +0.5 Chain.
  - +1 Consumable slot.
- **Item rarity in the shop:** Tools rare and pricey, consumables common. A shop never offers two of the same item.

## Parking lot (wild ideas, not in the set yet)

- **Mirror**: flip the table left-to-right for one shot.
- **Felt Brush**: paint a strip of slick or slow cloth for the rest of the table.
- **Echo Shot**: after the balls stop, the same hit replays from wherever the cue ball ended up.
- **Magnifier**: one ball is double size for a shot.
- **Time Bubble**: a circle where balls move at half speed.
- **Chalk Line**: draw a line; every ball that crosses it this shot adds Chain.
- **Phantom Ball**: a temporary ball that exists for one hit, a free combo starter.
