# Perk pass: scoring with Value = 10 + 2 × number

Pay = Value × Chain × Heat × multipliers. Flat bonuses are added to Value
before anything multiplies, so they scale with Chain and Heat too.

## The rule this pass follows

**Flat bonuses favour low balls; multipliers favour high balls.**
+35 nearly triples a 1-ball (12 → 47) but adds under 90% to a 15 (40 → 75).
×2 adds +12 to the 1 and +40 to the 15. Solid Perks lean on flat bonuses,
stripe and high-ball Perks lean on multipliers, so both builds stay worth playing.

Reference values: solids average 18, stripes average 34, all balls except the 8 average 26.

## Heat Perks (the new Heat family)

Heat is read when the shot starts, so you know what applies while you aim.

| Perk | Works when | Effect | Replaced |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold Start | Heat 0 | every sink ×2 | Regular Customer (reroll discount) |
| Simmer | below Heat 3 | shots start with +1 Chain | Long Night (Chain per shot taken) |
| Hot Hand | Heat 3+ | every ball +30 | (moved to the Heat family) |
| Inferno | On fire (Heat 5+) | every sink ×2 | Travel Light (empty slots) |
| Stay Hot | On fire (Heat 5+) | a miss costs 1 Heat, not half | its old hot-pocket rule |

The Thermal Cue still starts with Stay Hot, which now suits it.
