# Tables brainstorm: make every table play differently

**The problem.** Today most tables differ by a scoring rule (+0.5 Chain, ×1.5, +25 per ball) and maybe one physics tweak. You read the rule, then play the same game. Each table should change *what a good shot looks like*.

**Rules for every idea here**

1. **The prediction line still tells the truth.** Anything that moves follows a fixed pattern in time (a sweep, an orbit, a timer), so the forecast simulates it exactly like the balls. No randomness mid-shot.
2. **Moving things move in shot time.** They only move while balls are rolling, plus one step between shots. So aiming is never a reflex test, and pausing freezes them too.
3. **One big idea per table,** shown on the table itself (a moving rail, a creature, a gate), not just in text.
4. **Scoring rules come second.** We do a balance pass afterwards.

---

## The building blocks

### 1. Moving pockets
- **Sliding pockets:** a side pocket slides back and forth along its rail.
- **Orbiting pockets:** one pocket circles a point in the middle of the table.
- **Swapping pockets:** two pockets trade places after every shot.
- **Drains:** a hole in the felt (not at a rail) that slowly grows each shot.

### 2. Moving objects
- **Sweeper:** a bar that rotates around the centre like a clock hand.
- **Shuttles:** bumpers that slide between two points.
- **Gates:** short walls that open and close on a beat.
- **Conveyor strip:** a band of cloth that carries balls sideways.
- **Turntable:** a round patch that spins balls rolling across it.

### 3. Enemies: Sharks (pool hustlers, fittingly)
A **Shark** is a creature on the felt with **hit points (2–3)**.
- **Hit it** with any ball to knock off a point. **Knock it out** for bonus points and money.
- **Ignore it** and every few shots it acts: it **swallows a ball** (gone for this table), steals points, or bites Chalk.
- A Shark is solid like a ball, so the prediction line shows the hits.

Kinds:
- **Pool Shark:** drifts towards the ball nearest a pocket and swallows a ball every 3 shots.
- **Card Sharp:** sits in front of a pocket and blocks it until knocked out.
- **Loan Shark:** steals $2 every shot it lives; knocking it out pays back everything it took.
- **Hammerhead:** charges across the table between shots. Anything in its path gets knocked away.
- **Leech:** latches onto a ball. That ball scores half until another ball hits it free.

### 4. Terrain
- **Ice patch:** no friction inside the zone.
- **Sand trap:** balls slow sharply inside the zone.
- **Boost pad:** balls rolling over it speed up in its direction.
- **One-way rails:** cushions balls pass through one way only.
- **Wind lanes:** strips where balls drift one way.
- **Islands:** a solid block in the middle of the table, with cushions on all sides.

### 5. Table shape
- **Round table** with six pockets around the edge.
- **L-shaped table** (a corner cut out), so banks change completely.
- **Narrow waist:** the long rails pinch in at the middle.
- **Split table:** a low wall across the middle with one gap.

### 6. Change over a table
- **Rising tide:** the playable area shrinks each shot.
- **Shifting tilt:** the slope changes direction every shot (shown).
- **Night cycle:** pockets light up one at a time; only lit pockets score.
- **Restock:** a new ball rolls onto the table every few shots.

## Every table, reworked

The current scoring rules can stay as small extras; the new idea is the headline.

| Table | Now | Reworked: the big idea |
|---|---|---|
| **The Local** | Forgives your first miss | Stays plain on purpose: the tutorial table. Keeps the forgiveness |
| **Frozen Lake** | Slick cloth, cushions add Chain | **Ice patches** in two corners and a **cracking centre**: a big hole opens in the middle after 4 shots |
| **Spring Street** | Lively rails, side pockets boarded | **Shuttle bumpers** slide along both long rails; the side pockets open and shut on a beat |
| **The Drift** | Balls drift right | **Wind lanes** that flip direction every shot, with an arrow showing which way |
| **Old Slate** | Heavy cloth, +25 a ball | **Sand traps** in a ring around the rack; slow, thoughtful positional play |
| **The Back Room** | Narrow pockets | **Card Sharp** blocks the best pocket until you knock it out (3 hits) |
| **Pinball Parlor** | Four bumpers | **Real pinball:** spinning bumpers that fling balls faster, plus a **boost-pad** lane |
| **Twin Posts** | Two bumpers in the middle | **Shuttles:** the two posts slide towards and away from each other |
| **The Sinkhole** | Centre pulls balls in | **Drain:** a hole in the middle that grows each shot. Sinking into it scores ×2 but costs Heat |
| **The Hump** | A rise pushes balls away | **Shifting tilt:** the rise moves to a new spot every shot |
| **Rotation Night** | Hit the lowest ball first | **Night cycle:** one pocket lit at a time, moving round the table |
| **The Magnet Room** | Pockets pull, balls worth less | **Orbiting pocket:** one pocket circles the table on a fixed path |
| **The Smoke Room** | No spin | **Pool Shark** circles in the smoke; it swallows a ball every 3 shots unless you knock it out |
| **Black Ice** | No friction, lively cushions | **Turntables:** two spinning discs curve every ball that crosses them |
| **The Tilted Table** | Slope shown with dots | **Conveyor strip** across the middle carries balls towards one rail |
| **Two Pockets** | Only two pockets score | **Swapping pockets:** the two scoring pockets trade places with others after each shot |
| **Closing Time** | Pockets narrow each shot | **Rising tide:** the table itself shrinks each shot, and the walls move in |
| **The Iron Rack** | Heavy balls, +30 | **Island:** a solid block in the centre to bank around, plus heavy balls |
| **Lights Out** | No hot pocket | **Gates** open and close on a beat; the line shows when they will be open |
| **The Hustler** | Heavy cue, house skims 15% | **Loan Shark** takes $2 every shot until you knock it out; it pays it all back when it goes |
| **The Clockwork Table** | Pockets in clock order | Keep the clock order, add a **Sweeper**: a clock hand that rotates one step each shot |
| **The Grand Final** | The 8 goes last | **Boss fight:** the **Great White** (8 hits) swallows a ball every 2 shots; sink the 8 last *and* beat it for the big finish |

**New shapes** (could replace the weakest tables above): a **Round Table**, an **L-shaped table** and a **Split table**.

## How this fits the rest of the game

- **Isotopes and Elements get new targets.** For example, an Element "Sharks take double damage", a Catalyst "Harpoon: hit a Shark from anywhere", and Volatile balls that explode on Sharks.
- **Pause becomes tactical.** Pause the shot as the Sweeper comes round, then use a Catalyst to slip a ball past it.
- **Side bets get new kinds:** "Knock out the Shark this table" or "Sink through a moving pocket".
- **Order through a run:** tables 1–3 use one simple mover each, tables 4–6 add Sharks and terrain, and tables 7–9 combine two ideas.

## Questions for you

1. **Sharks:** should they swallow balls (lost for the table, back next table) or steal points or money? Or mix it by kind, as above?
2. **How wild should shapes get?** A round or L-shaped table is a big physics job but very fresh.
3. **Which 5 should come first?** My pick: **Pool Shark** (Smoke Room), **Shuttle bumpers** (Spring Street), **Orbiting pocket** (Magnet Room), **Sweeper** (Clockwork) and the **Great White** final.
